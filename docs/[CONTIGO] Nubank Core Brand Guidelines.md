LOGO — SYMBOL 

**Logo Container** 

The logo in a container is our preferred version and should be used consistently. 

One of the key benefits of using the container is that it enhances the presence of our purple, making it more prominent and recognizable across applications. 

The tapered qualities of the line, paired with geometric proportions, create a beautiful tension between elegance and structure. 

As an ambigram, our logo is legible when viewed from different angles, allowing for flexibility in application.
